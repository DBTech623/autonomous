# Autonomous

## Evidence access ledger

**Status:** Canon control for Book I scene drafting  
**Source:** Completed act map and scene maps, Chapters 1–32  
**Last updated:** 2026-07-24

## Purpose

This ledger controls who can see each piece of evidence, how they obtain it, what it proves, and where it stops. It prevents Naomi from acquiring intelligence-agency access by convenience and prevents Malcolm's clearance from becoming unlimited.

The reader may receive a simpler explanation than the one recorded here. The underlying access path must still hold.

## Access rules

1. Naomi begins with public material, her outlet's own data, observation, interviews, or documents a source deliberately gives her.
2. Naomi can recognize a pattern without being able to explain its machinery. Technical sources interpret records; they do not solve the investigation for her.
3. Malcolm sees only material covered by his NSA assignment, the OSSI audit compartment, or access another authorized person explicitly grants.
4. Malcolm may compare Naomi's published evidence with classified timing in his own mind. He cannot move her unpublished source material into government systems without her agreement.
5. Corporate and government records have custody trails. Screenshots and anonymous files create leads; authenticated originals support publication or official findings.
6. No single record proves Polaris exists. The case emerges from timing, repeated architecture, missing authorization, corporate capability, and human consequence.
7. Each major disclosure must answer two questions on the page: why this person can see it, and what remains hidden.

## Naomi's technical competence

Naomi is a technically literate investigative reporter, not a hacker or network engineer.

She can:

- search public routing history when given an event window, network range, or autonomous-system number;
- compare timestamps across public archives, filings, archived pages, platform analytics, and source documents;
- notice repeated identifiers and ask informed questions;
- preserve files, metadata, and version histories well enough to maintain a reporting trail;
- explain the human consequence of a technical pattern in plain language.

She needs a specialist to:

- interpret whether a BGP change is routine failover, a leak, deliberate traffic engineering, or something indeterminate;
- see private carrier routing, customer relationships, internal instructions, or proprietary topology;
- validate code or system architecture;
- distinguish a suggestive technical pattern from a publishable causal claim.

Her reporting skill is the connective tissue. Sources possess narrow pieces. Naomi sees that the pieces belong in the same story.

## Malcolm's access ceiling

Malcolm's government position gives him breadth but not freedom.

- NSA telemetry lets him see data already collected for authorized missions. It does not give him arbitrary access to private companies, domestic newsrooms, or every allied system.
- OSSI grants a purpose-bound audit compartment. Cate and Torres can narrow sources, queries, exports, and terminology.
- Vale controls what the official visiting team sees. Malcolm's later access exists only because Adrian preserved a dormant identity.
- The Aurora archive remains sealed until the story earns a lawful or deliberately opened path.
- Information from Naomi stays outside classified systems unless they agree on an authentication and attribution route.

## Drafting test

Before a character uses evidence in a scene, confirm:

- The record has a named custodian.
- The character has a stated access route.
- The record contains the fact being inferred.
- A specialist interprets technical material when necessary.
- The scene states or dramatizes what the evidence cannot prove.
- Moving the evidence into a new institution creates a custody, source-protection, or classification problem.
- The next discovery follows from work already shown rather than arriving because the plot needs it.

## Continuity pass results

The scene maps now use the following fixed access chains:

- Naomi's opening investigation moves from newsroom analytics to public routing history, source interpretation, a cropped carrier extract, and corporate records.
- Luka's election files carry Civic Count authentication, preservation-register checks, and sending-device and receiving-server timestamps.
- Elif's Vardonia order reaches Naomi as a certified redacted copy released by parliamentary counsel.
- OSSI's isotope test relies on data already available to the audit rather than direct entry into unrelated private systems.
- Daniel reaches the old integration package through time-limited compliance access. Vale detects the out-of-scope retrieval.
- Daniel's original upload remains with newsroom counsel. Malcolm receives a hash-verified encrypted copy that stays outside government systems.
- Naomi's Istanbul health information comes from the conference app, a public supplier certificate, and named hospital and municipal contacts.
- Zeynep's manifest access comes from her parliamentary scheduling account.
- Post-attack device evidence reaches Zeynep through delegation counsel and a formal victim-liaison briefing.
- The earlier Markou manifesto fragment survives through an independent archivist's copy of a publishing cache, with headers and a hash.

No scene now requires Naomi to hack a system, possess unexplained technical access, or know an operational fact that belongs only in another viewpoint. No scene gives Malcolm unrestricted government visibility or permits him to move Naomi's source material into OSSI without a custody decision.
