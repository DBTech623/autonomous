# Autonomous — Reimagined Structure (Outline, Take Four: Interleaving + Compression + Aurora Atmosphere)

**Status:** HISTORICAL / SUPERSEDED (updated 2026-08-13). This was the planning outline for the restructuring; the reimagined reading manuscript (`Autonomous_Book_1_Reimagined_Reading_Manuscript.md`) is now complete and is the authoritative text. Kept as a record of the restructuring rationale (interleaving, compression, Aurora-atmosphere levers) — not an active plan.

**Revision history:**
- **Take One** regrouped the existing 32 chapters into five movements without challenging chapter boundaries — resequencing at the margins.
- **Take Two** rebuilt Movements II–III at the scene level using full-prose reading, relocating scenes across old chapter boundaries and filling a structural gap (Naomi absent for three straight chapters).
- **Take Three** restored compression as an independent lever on top of Take Two's scene order — interleaving fixes how long a thread runs unbroken; compression fixes how much time is spent re-running a scene shape the reader has already learned. Both levers active.
- **Take Four (this version)** adds a third lever: deepen the reader's *felt* access to what Aurora cost Malcolm, earlier in the book, without revealing anything Malcolm himself doesn't yet know. This is atmosphere and interiority, not new plot information — checked against `Aurora_to_Polaris_Canon_History.md` to confirm it doesn't leak Book II material (Varga, the Constellation, the extraction operation, who altered the configuration — none of that is decided even at the trilogy-bible level yet, so none of it can appear here regardless of restructuring).

**Citation key:** `ChNN.Sx` = chapter and scene number. **[REUSE]** = as-is or lightly trimmed (<10%). **[COMPRESS]** = meaningful cut, target percentage given. **[RESEQUENCE]** = moved/interleaved, no new prose. **[NEW]** = new material. **[TEXTURE]** = a small addition (1–3 sentences to a short paragraph) layered into an already-REUSE/COMPRESS section — not a new scene, an enrichment of an existing one. Hard constraint unchanged: no character fate, plot event, or world-canon change — structure, sequencing, pacing, and now interiority depth, are what's open.

## What Book I already discloses about Aurora/Moldova (confirmed against canon, not assumed)

By Chapter 22, the reader and Naomi both learn: Aurora's purpose (cross-domain escalation-prevention AI, mandatory human authorization), the operation name (Steady Lantern), the setting (a Transnistria border crisis), and the outcome (seventeen civilian deaths — hospitals, dispatch, a stalled rail evacuation — officially blamed on Malcolm's safeguards). Chapters 20, 23, and 24 add Sam's discovery of the configuration mismatch and Cate's admission she knew. **This is Book I payoff, not Book II material — the reveal ladder already lands it.**

What's genuinely reserved for later books, per `Aurora_to_Polaris_Canon_History.md` §14 (Open Questions) and §15 (Continuity Rules): Varga, the Constellation, Adrian's role in specifying the extraction, and — notably — **who physically altered the configuration is explicitly undecided even at the trilogy-planning level**, not just withheld from Book I's reader. So this pass cannot invent an answer to that question; it can only deepen what Malcolm already carries emotionally, within what he already knows (guilt, the death toll, his reassignment) plus what he learns on schedule (the configuration mismatch, on the existing Ch20/23/24 reveal points).

**Guardrail for every addition below:** no new facts. No Varga, Constellation, Steady Lantern name, death count, or sabotage implication earlier than their existing reveal points (the word "Moldova" is already usable from Ch3 onward; "Aurora" by name from Ch5 onward, per the existing text — that pattern is preserved, just deepened). These are behavioral tells, sense-memory, and weight — the reader should feel Malcolm is carrying something specific and heavy well before Ch20, without learning any more of what it is than the current text already discloses at each point.

## Net effect across all three levers

- **Interleaving** (Take Two): 13 scenes relocated across old chapter boundaries; Naomi's 3-chapter absence closed.
- **Compression** (Take Three): five targeted cuts of 25–50% at recurrences of the "question → institutional deflection → sharper question" shape (III.3, III.15, III.16, III.21, III.26), leaving scenes that do new work at full length.
- **Aurora atmosphere** (Take Four): three small texture additions (I.3, I.6, II.1 — none more than a few sentences) plus one repurposed new scene (II.2, now doing double duty as both the Naomi-gap fill and an external confirmation that Malcolm is carrying something specific) in the stretch where the book currently gives the reader the least felt access to what Aurora costs him. Ch6's existing flashback material is deliberately left untouched — it's already doing this job well; adding more near it would dilute rather than strengthen it.
