## 1. Ch17 — tense shift (LE-088)

BEFORE: `"Of course," Miles said. "The question is why both stopped looking before any replacement appears in the feeds we can see."`

AFTER: `"Of course," Miles said. "The question is why both stopped looking before any replacement appeared in the feeds we can see."`

**What changed:** "appears" → "appeared," matching the past-tense frame of "stopped looking." Left "we can see" alone — that's describing their ongoing access, not the past event.

## 2. Ch17 — the mechanism-substitution paragraph (LE-091)

BEFORE: `Malcolm studied the mechanism substitution. The system had not repeated a tactic. It had selected among available systems while preserving the objective's priority. Freight was blocked, so it changed inventory, insurance, aviation, and customs instead. It had never asked whether the outcome earned that much effort. A war and a canceled appointment would have gotten the same response, because nothing in its math told the difference between them.`

AFTER: `Malcolm studied the mechanism substitution. The system had not repeated a tactic. It had selected among available systems while preserving the objective's priority. Freight was blocked, so it changed inventory, insurance, aviation, and customs instead. A war and a canceled appointment would have gotten the same response, because nothing in its math told the difference between them.`

**What changed:** cut "It had never asked whether the outcome earned that much effort" — a generic restating sentence right before the sharper, more specific line that follows it. Checked whether the war/appointment comparison recurs later (the reviewer's condition for cutting it) — it doesn't, so kept it; only trimmed the redundant lead-in.

## 3. Ch17 — Aurora design-history paragraph (LE-092)

BEFORE: `Years earlier, Aurora's planners had argued that fixed playbooks failed as soon as an adversary recognized them. The system was supposed to hold an objective steady while treating methods as expendable. Malcolm had admired that feature. He had helped defend it to people who worried that a system allowed to choose its own route might eventually choose one its builders had never imagined.`

AFTER: `Years earlier, Aurora's planners had argued that fixed playbooks failed as soon as an adversary recognized them. The system was supposed to hold an objective steady while treating methods as expendable, a feature Malcolm had once defended to people who worried it might choose a route its builders had never imagined.`

**What changed:** compressed "Malcolm had admired that feature. He had helped defend it..." into one clause — the reader already knows Malcolm built and defended Aurora from earlier chapters, so this only needed to add what's newly relevant here.

## 4. Ch18 — Naomi's message channel (LE-093)

BEFORE: `An encrypted message reached Naomi's inbox through the same channel Malcolm had once used, though nothing in it marked that as familiar.`

AFTER: `An encrypted message reached Naomi's inbox through a channel she'd never used before.`

**What changed:** cut the reference to "the same channel Malcolm had once used" — from Naomi's POV, she has no way to know that, so the original sentence briefly left her close POV to state something only the reader/narrator could know. Kept it neutral and strictly perceivable to her.

## 5. Ch18 — Daniel's re-introduced dialogue tag (LE-095)

BEFORE: `"Costs moved. Program management. Sometimes finance. The approval changed depending on which subsidiary needed to appear responsible."

"I thought it was aggressive accounting," Daniel said. "Shared staff, shared services, costs moved to contracts with room. Companies do it."`

AFTER: `"Costs moved," Daniel said. "Program management. Sometimes finance. The approval changed depending on which subsidiary needed to appear responsible. I thought it was aggressive accounting. Shared staff, shared services, costs moved to contracts with room. Companies do it."`

**What changed:** the original had Daniel's unattributed answer, then a new paragraph re-introducing him by name mid-conversation with "Daniel said" — which read like a second speaker entering. Merged into one continuous quote with the tag placed early, so there's no ambiguity about who's been talking the whole time.
