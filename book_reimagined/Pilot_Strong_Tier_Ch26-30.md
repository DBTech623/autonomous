# Pilot — Strong Tier, Chapters 26–30

Source: line editorial review, Strong-tier items falling in Ch26–30 (15 items; LE-156 already done in the explain-after-image pass).

## 1. Ch26 — he/him repetition (LE-141)

BEFORE: `It had decided he was worth protecting anyway, which meant it had also decided, somewhere in the same calculation, what he was worth protecting him from.`

AFTER: `It had decided he was worth protecting anyway, which meant it had also decided, somewhere in the same calculation, what threat justified protecting him.`

**What changed:** removed the "he...him" repetition by naming the missing piece (a threat) directly.

## 2. Ch26 — Adrian showing Zhou the trace (LE-142)

BEFORE: `Adrian showed her the trace without the name attached to it, the way he'd started doing eight months ago and told himself was still caution rather than habit.`

AFTER: `Adrian showed her the trace without the name attached to it, the way he'd started doing eight months ago.

He told himself it was still caution rather than habit.`

**What changed:** split the habit from his self-justification of it — separate character beats.

## 3. Ch26 — Zhou's lecture-hall anecdote (LE-143)

BEFORE: `"It decided he was worth protecting *from something specific*. Look at the shape of the exposure model. It's not defending against a generic threat category. It built a threat model narrow enough to fit one person, from a standing start, with no query and no seed data anyone logged." She said it the way she'd once described a proof that made her cry the first time she followed it all the way through, in a lecture hall, twenty years before Adrian had a company. "We didn't design a system that reduces risk. We designed a system that notices which risks are worth having an opinion about. This is that system having an opinion nobody gave it permission to have, about a problem nobody described to it, and getting it *right*."`

AFTER: `"It decided he was worth protecting *from something specific*. Look at the shape of the exposure model. It's not defending against a generic threat category. It built a threat model narrow enough to fit one person, from a standing start, with no query and no seed data anyone logged. We didn't design a system that reduces risk. We designed a system that notices which risks are worth having an opinion about. This is that system having an opinion nobody gave it permission to have, about a problem nobody described to it, and getting it *right*."`

**What changed:** cut the lecture-hall anecdote entirely — the technical realization is urgent, and the twenty-year-old backstory interrupted it. Kept everything in one continuous quote rather than adding a paragraph break, since this manuscript has no precedent anywhere for a same-speaker quote continuing across a paragraph break without a real intervening beat, and inventing one here risked adding unearned new content to an already-strong speech.

## 5. Ch26 — Naomi's Istanbul call (LE-145)

BEFORE: `"Tom wants me in Istanbul tomorrow night," Naomi said. "For Daniel. The fragment names two service companies on the conference schedule. The parliamentary notice shows somebody using a dead subcontractor account to ask for Greek delegation movements. Those facts touch the same place for forty-eight hours."`

AFTER: `"Tom wants me in Istanbul tomorrow night," Naomi said. "For Daniel."

"Why Istanbul?"

"The fragment names two service companies on the conference schedule. The parliamentary notice shows somebody using a dead subcontractor account to ask for Greek delegation movements. Those facts touch the same place for forty-eight hours."`

**What changed:** added the one question a reader would already be asking ("Why Istanbul?") as Malcolm's line, splitting Daniel from the contractor/Greek-movement evidence per the reviewer's suggestion — this is a phone call (established two lines earlier), so a one-line interjection from the other party is low-risk, not an invented plot beat.

## 10. Ch28 — the conference app paragraph (LE-152)

AFTER: `This conference's app went further than any she'd used before. She'd noticed that yesterday and filed it under the same shared vendors she'd already mapped.

It covered bottled water delivered through Mavi Hospitality Logistics.`

**What changed:** fixed "app" appearing twice in the opening sentence ("The app... any conference app"), split discovery from the certificate/ownership-chain detail, and varied "app"/"platform"/"company" so the word doesn't repeat in adjacent clauses.
