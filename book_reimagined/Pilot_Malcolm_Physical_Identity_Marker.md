# Pilot — Malcolm's Physical-Identity Marker (Ch5)

Source: `Autonomous_Developmental_Editorial_Review.docx`, MEDIUM item. Malcolm is locked canon as African American (`Character_Physical_Descriptions.md`), but the manuscript never places that on the page anywhere — confirmed by grepping the full manuscript for race/skin-tone language: Naomi gets one at her Ch1 intro ("brown skin, dark hair"), Malcolm never does.

**Placement:** Ch5 ("The Redaction"), the badge-photograph passage — the review's own suggested spot, since it already inventories everything that changed about Malcolm in four years (hair, glasses, face, jaw, posture). One clause naming what *didn't* change fits the existing rhythm of the passage rather than reading as an insert.

**Why this phrasing:** matches the locked bible language ("dark brown skin") and Naomi's parallel construction at her own introduction, so the two leads get equivalent treatment rather than Malcolm's being conspicuously later or more elaborate. Framed as the one constant against everything else in the passage that's listed as changed — ordinary observation, not an explanation of significance.

**BEFORE:**

> Malcolm looked down at the badge. The photograph was four years old. His hair was shorter then, cropped close the same way he still kept it, though gray had gotten into the sides since. He hadn't needed glasses yet. His face was fuller, and nothing had happened in Moldova yet.

**AFTER:**

> Malcolm looked down at the badge. The photograph was four years old. Dark brown skin, the one thing the four years hadn't touched. His hair was shorter then, cropped close the same way he still kept it, though gray had gotten into the sides since. He hadn't needed glasses yet. His face was fuller, and nothing had happened in Moldova yet.
