# Pilot — Cold Read Findings #3 (Cate's voice) + #4 (Ch21–22/25 access loop)

**Source:** `Cold_Read_Report.md` §4/§9 item 4 — "three chapters of 'Malcolm asks, Cate deflects' cover largely the same beat"; and §6/§9 item 3 — Cate (among others) argues in the same clipped register as the leads.

**User decision:** trim + re-voice, treated as one combined pass on Cate specifically (the other four characters — Adrian, Torres, Tom, Zeynep — are a separate pass, see below).

## Honest finding after a close read

Having read all five Cate/Malcolm scenes across Ch21, 22, and 25 in full, this isn't three chapters of the same beat. Each scene does distinct work:
- **Ch21 office** — the official ask, rebuffed on need-to-know grounds.
- **Ch21 elevator** — consequences of forcing a written denial; ends in agreeing to a leadership review.
- **Ch21 OSSI-counsel** — a different specific angle (sanitized extraction), plus introduces the Markou/Istanbul lead. Not Malcolm-facing at all.
- **Ch22 leadership** — the official channel closes via bureaucratic concurrence vote.
- **Ch22 aftermath** — the emotional break: Cate's personal stake surfaces ("Was I one of the mistakes?").
- **Ch25 confrontation** — Malcolm goes around the process, gets caught, and gets the first real admission ("The original inquiry found configuration discrepancies").

Cate's own phrasing already escalates across these: rule-citing (Ch21) → oblique personal admission (Ch22: "Because the country still needs it") → direct factual confession (Ch25). That's real differentiation already on the page, not something that needs to be invented. Cutting a whole scene here would cost the Markou setup, the leadership-vote payoff, or the "Was I one of the mistakes" beat — none of which are redundant with each other.

**What's actually cuttable:** one clearly redundant volley in the Ch21 office scene, where two lines just restate the same "no access" point a second way.

## Change

BEFORE:
> "Assign the comparison to a cleared analyst."
>
> "The compartment is dormant. Custodial access. They can preserve it, not analyze it."
>
> "Then restore one analyst."
>
> "That reopens the compartment."

AFTER:
> "Assign the comparison to a cleared analyst."
>
> "The compartment is dormant. Restoring one to analyze it reopens the compartment."

**What changed:** merged two response beats into one — "preserve it, not analyze it" and "restoring one reopens the compartment" were saying the same thing twice. No information lost, four lines become two.

**Self-audit:** no em-dashes introduced. No plot/character content cut, only a repeated logical point.

## Recommendation on scope

I don't think this scene complex needs (or would benefit from) more cutting or Cate re-voicing beyond this one trim — the material is doing real work and her voice already varies with what's actually happening in each scene. The genuine "everyone sounds the same" problem the report flagged is more real for **Adrian, Torres, Tom, and Zeynep**, who I haven't touched yet. Suggest treating finding #3 as its own pass covering those four next, rather than forcing more changes onto a Cate arc that's already working.
