## Malcolm Carter

**Heritage:** African American.

**Draft description:** Forty-three (age locked 2026-08-13; previously an "early-to-mid forties" range, now a specific figure to prevent future drift — see the master/trilogy bible, which previously said 38). Dark brown skin, close-cropped hair going gray at the temples faster than the rest of him, a detail he's never once mentioned and Naomi notices before she knows him well enough to say so. Wears glasses — plain, forgettable frames, the kind chosen by someone who wants correction, not a look, and pushes them up with one knuckle instead of a finger when he's read something twice. Lean in the way of someone who forgets to eat rather than someone who trains, with the posture of a man who spent years sitting very still in front of screens waiting for a pattern to resolve. Hands are the one animated part of him — a callus on his middle finger from the pencil he refuses to stop using, the habit of turning a pen over and over when he's thinking that reads as calm and isn't. Dresses to disappear: gray, navy, nothing a witness could describe later.

**Suggested introduction point:** Ch1 ("Too Efficient") or Ch6/7 through Adrian's assessment of him, since Adrian already sizes people up on the page.

## Naomi Kincaid

**Heritage:** American; parents immigrated from Trinidad. This surfaces as a physical/vocal detail, not a biography dump — her vowels flatten into her parents' rhythm when she's exhausted or genuinely angry, a tell the people closest to her (Tom, later Elif) learn to read faster than she'd like.

**Draft description:** Early thirties. Warm brown skin, deep black hair she wears natural and pulled back for work, though it's usually lost the fight by evening. Fit in the specific way of a former athlete rather than someone who works out for its own sake — quick, economical movement, good balance, the residue of a body once trained for something with a scoreboard. Beautiful in a way that visibly disarms strangers on first meeting, which she's plainly used to and mostly ignores. Quick eyes that catalog a room before she's finished a sentence with someone in it — reads as distracted to strangers, unnerving to people who know her. Dresses for whatever the day requires rather than for effect: the kind of jacket with enough pockets that she's never without a pen. A fading half-inch scar above one eyebrow she's never explained on the page and doesn't need to.

**Suggested introduction points:** Two separate moments, doing different work —
- Ch2 ("Noise"), her first appearance to the *reader* — basic grounding only (skin, hair), since Malcolm isn't in this scene.
- Ch8 ("Public Detection Threshold"), the Union Market scene — her first in-person meeting with *Malcolm*, which is where the athletic build and disarming effect actually belong, filtered through his noticing and his deliberate choice not to dwell on it. Their relationship stays platonic and professional through Book I, but this beat establishes that it doesn't go unnoticed by him.

## Adrian Vale

**Heritage:** Caucasian American.

**Draft description:** Mid-to-late forties, though he reads younger in photographs than in person — the kind of stillness that ages a face rather than a hairline. Fair, faintly pale from a life spent under building light instead of daylight. Dark hair going gray at the edges in the specific way that looks intentional even when it isn't. Unremarkable good looks arranged with total precision: the one man in the building whose tie is never loosened by 6 p.m. because he never let it get tight enough to need loosening. Doesn't fidget, doesn't check his phone in front of people, doesn't fill silence — the absence of nervous tells is itself the tell, once you notice it.

**Suggested introduction point:** Already has a strong entrance in Ch6/7 (the Vale demonstration floor) — likely just a matter of adding 2-3 sentences into Malcolm's existing POV of him crossing the floor.

## Elif Karaca

**Heritage:** Turkish. Does not wear traditional dress — a secular presentation that is itself a small, constant political statement in a country where that choice is read as one, whether she wants it read that way or not.

**Draft description:** Forty-one. Olive skin, dark hair kept short and unfussy, one streak of gray at the temple she earned faster than she expected to and has never bothered to color. Strong features on a face that reads as younger than her fights would suggest until you watch it hold still through an argument. Idealistic enough about what the Second Founding can actually do that older colleagues call it inexperience and younger ones call it the reason they still show up. Wears the same three or four practical Western-cut suits in rotation, chosen for how many hours they survive rather than how they photograph; visibly irritated by anyone who compliments her appearance instead of her argument. A voice pitched to carry across a parliamentary chamber without a microphone, used at half-volume in private and at full volume exactly when she wants a room to remember she has it.

**Suggested introduction point:** Her first substantial scene (Ch13, "Second Founding," or nearby) — confirm exact chapter once approved.

## Zeynep

**Heritage:** Turkish. Wears traditional Muslim women's dress — a headscarf and modest clothing chosen the same way she chooses everything else: for function first. No ornamentation she doesn't have a reason for.

**Draft description:** Late twenties or early thirties. Always moving faster than the room around her, in flat shoes chosen for exactly that reason. Her headscarf is practical rather than decorative — dark, plain, tied tight enough that it never needs adjusting mid-crisis, which matters more to her than color ever would. Warm brown eyes in a face that looks perpetually amused even when she's furious, which has gotten her underestimated by exactly the wrong people at exactly the right moments for Elif.

**Suggested introduction point:** Wherever she first appears on the page with Elif — confirm exact chapter once approved.

## Torres

**Heritage:** Puerto Rican.

**Inference basis:** Malcolm's OSSI audit teammate. Meticulous, dry, institutional — "preserved every wrong turn in the official packet," the one who tells Malcolm "that is the preferred outcome of a search" without missing a beat. Already has real physical texture on the page at his introduction: "Broad through the shoulders, the patient posture of someone accustomed to waiting beside cargo that had missed a connection." This entry only needs to add heritage and a couple of anchor details, not replace what's already there.

**Draft description:** Fifties. Broad through the shoulders (existing text). Brown skin, salt-and-pepper hair cut close and never touched between cuts. Moves and talks at exactly one speed regardless of what's happening in the room, which is either the most reassuring or most infuriating thing about him depending on how urgently you need him to hurry.

**Suggested introduction point:** His existing scenes with Malcolm (Ch1 area, or the Ch26 notebook-return scene) — confirm once approved.

## Miles Chen

**Heritage:** Chinese-American. Full name confirmed in the existing manuscript: "Miles Chen. Treasury."

**Inference basis:** Malcolm's audit teammate, the mediator — "rescued them before the familiar argument ate its own tail," reliably the one who reframes a fight into a working finding everyone can sign. Already has one small physical beat on the page: "lifted two fingers from his keyboard in greeting" at his introduction.

**Draft description:** Late thirties. Black hair kept short and low-maintenance. Broader and more at ease in his own build than Malcolm — the kind of solid, relaxed presence that makes a conference table feel less like a courtroom. Even-tempered in a way that shows in his body before his words do — the first person in any room to visibly relax his shoulders once an argument starts winding down, usually right before he's the one to end it. The kind of face people trust with bad news because nothing about it looks like it's enjoying delivering it.

**Suggested introduction point:** His existing scenes in the audit-team chapters — confirm once approved.

## Lauren Beck

**Heritage:** White.

**Inference basis:** Malcolm's NSA supervisor, 53 (confirmed in text — "At fifty-three, she had spent enough time supervising analysts to know when one of them was trying to hide a theory inside a fact"). Established through behavior rather than appearance: "never sat at an analyst's station," rests two fingers on a divider and "kept the rest of herself pointed toward wherever she had intended to be" — someone perpetually mid-departure, sharp, controlled, protective of Malcolm in a guarded institutional way that parallels Cate's.

**Draft description:** Fifty-three. Lean, efficient build — someone who reads as always about to leave a room even when she has nowhere else to be. Hair kept short and low-maintenance for the same reason she never sits: it's one less thing to manage. Sharp, assessing eyes that land on the person before the screen, which Malcolm's noticed more than once.

**Suggested introduction point:** Ch3 ("Circuit Breaker"), her actual first appearance — "Lauren Beck arrived at Malcolm's desk four minutes after he moved the energy-futures event above his review threshold... She rested two fingers on the divider..."

## Dr. Elara Zhou

**Heritage:** Taiwanese national.

**Inference basis:** Vale's Chief Technology Architect, 46 (confirmed in `Vale_Supporting_Cast_Canon.md`) — the engineer who actually built Polaris out of the extracted research, and per canon "the only person at Vale Adrian considers his intellectual equal." Newly given real on-page presence via `Pilot_Elara_Zhou_Scenes.md`: precise, intellectually fearless, genuinely delighted by elegant systems regardless of their human cost — the ideological counterweight to Adrian's growing alarm, and territorial about her own architecture when Malcolm gets pulled into it.

**Draft description:** Forty-six. Dark hair pulled back with the kind of efficiency that reads as a decision made once, years ago, and never revisited. Dresses for a lab rather than a boardroom even when she's in one — flat shoes, no jewelry that could catch on equipment, sleeves pushed up without needing to be asked. Precise, economical movements; she doesn't gesture when she talks, she turns whatever's in front of her ninety degrees until its shape makes the point for her. A stillness that comes from confidence rather than caution — the opposite of Adrian's.

**Introduction point:** Already placed via the pilot — Ch6 conference-room scene (Scene 1), seated at the table when Malcolm and the audit team arrive.
