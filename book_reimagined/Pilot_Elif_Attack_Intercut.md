# Pilot Draft — Direct intercut of the tunnel attack (Elif / Tunalı), Ch29

**Purpose:** Right now the attack that kills Elif is experienced entirely at a remove — Naomi and Zeynep watching vehicle icons vanish, a blast alert, a phone call confirming casualties. Tense, but the user wants the actual strike dramatized directly: Elif's POV inside vehicle four, intercut with Tunalı's team executing it, rather than only the aftermath as it reaches the outside world.

**Craft note on the exception this makes:** the book's method everywhere else is mediated, fragmented information — that's thematic, not incidental. This scene works as a deliberate one-time break from that method, not a change to it. It should end at the edge of the moment of death, not narrate through it — cutting away at impact keeps faith with how the book handles violence everywhere else (restrained, consequence-focused, not staged as action).

**Canon fidelity checked:** Tunalı's team still believes they're killing Markou — nothing here has him or his team realize otherwise, since canon leaves that unresolved in Book I. The attack stays confined to the vehicle/immediate detail per canon's "Conference Venue and Evacuation Geography" section (tunnel confines it, doesn't become a mass-casualty event). Callbacks used: Tunalı's "twenty-six years of watching water for smugglers" line from his Ch18/19 introduction; the tunnel/team details from the Ch26 planning scene (two men at the maintenance door, a demolitions specialist).

**Placement:** Ch29, inserted between "The convoy's last location remained blank." and "A blast alert entered the municipal feed." — i.e., at the exact moment Naomi's dread has fully built (vehicle icons gone, no network, blank location) but before any mediated echo of the event reaches the outside world. The scene cuts directly to what's actually happening, then cuts back out to the public feed picking up the aftermath.

**Open questions for you:**
- **Scene-break density.** Four short beats intercut with `* * *` reads as rapid crosscutting, which matches an action climax, but it's denser than the book's usual rhythm (each `* * *` elsewhere tends to mark a full scene, not a quick cut). Could thin the breaks if it reads choppy against the surrounding chapters.
- **Elif's last line.** "Zeynep has the coalition lists" is deliberately unsentimental — in character for her (direct, work-first even under duress, established via "Keep the procurement records outside Turkey" a few lines earlier in the existing text) rather than a swelling emotional beat. Flag if you want something warmer instead.
- **Tunalı's closing line** leans on the book's "systems decide, indifferent to who pays" theme rather than giving him an interior reaction to killing someone. If you want a beat of his own reaction (satisfaction, doubt, nothing) rather than a purely thematic closer, say so.
