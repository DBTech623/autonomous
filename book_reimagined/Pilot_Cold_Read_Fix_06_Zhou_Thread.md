## What this does and doesn't do

- **Pays off her exit line** ("I'll pull his sessions myself") instead of letting it dangle unaddressed — a reader who noticed her disappear now gets confirmation she followed through, not silence.
- **Shows independence, not allegiance.** She reports to neither Adrian nor Varga — she's now acting on her own, which is consistent with (and doesn't foreclose) a Book II arc where she isn't simply "Adrian's employee" or "Varga's asset."
- **Deliberately vague on specifics.** "Four hundred days" establishes scale (the mystery she's found is much older/bigger than Malcolm's visible involvement) without naming a cause, an origin system, or a plan — nothing here commits Book II to anything.
- **"Kept working" is the whole point** — she's still active, off-page, not gone. That's the fix for "drops out completely," not a resolution.
- Does not touch her allegiance, her nationality/leverage thread, or any Varga-channel material reserved for Book II.
