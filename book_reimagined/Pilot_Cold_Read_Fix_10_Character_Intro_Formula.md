# Pilot — Cold Read Finding #10: Character-introduction formula (round 2)

**Source:** `Cold_Read_Report.md` §6/§9 item 10, and the user's correction that the earlier line-review pass on this same issue didn't actually fix it — the underlying age/ethnicity/hair/habit checklist survived because the reference doc it was drafted from (`Character_Physical_Descriptions.md`) is itself written in that exact template, so the earlier pass varied placement and wording without breaking the underlying information structure.

## Fresh survey

Checked every named character's actual current introduction against the formula. Mixed result, not uniform:

**Already well-varied — models to copy from:** Torres ("rose and offered Malcolm a hand, broad through the shoulders, brown-skinned" — one detail folded into action, no age, no hair), Zhou (zero physical description at intro — pure voice/behavior), Reddick (zero physical description — introduced entirely via a phone call), Mara Voss ("a woman in a gray blazer" — clothing + behavior only), Zeynep (one detail folded into action, no age stated), Cate (one detail — hair — plus behavior, no age).

**Still checklist-shaped, despite the earlier pass:** Beck, Adrian, Daniel Cho, Naomi, Leila, Elif. These six get rewritten below, using the already-successful characters above as the technique model rather than inventing a new approach.

**User direction:** vary heritage handling per character rather than one blanket rule — keep it at introduction where it's already working or thematically load-bearing, defer or drop the checklist-label version where it isn't.

## 1. Lauren Beck (Ch3)

BEFORE:
> She did not sit. Beck never sat at an analyst's station. White, lean, hair kept short because it was one less thing to manage. She rested two fingers on the divider, leaned far enough to read the display, and kept the rest of herself pointed toward wherever she had intended to be.

AFTER:
> She did not sit. Beck never sat at an analyst's station. She rested two fingers on the divider and leaned far enough to read the display, hair cut short enough that maintaining it had stopped being a decision years ago, and kept the rest of herself pointed toward wherever she had intended to be.

**Heritage choice:** dropped "White" as a bare label. Her behavioral characterization (never sitting, always mid-departure) carries the scene; whiteness isn't thematically load-bearing for her role the way heritage is for Elif or Daniel Cho. Folded the hair detail into the existing action sentence instead of giving it its own checklist clause.

## 2. Adrian Vale (Ch6/7)

BEFORE:
> Adrian Vale crossed the floor without the cluster of aides Malcolm associated with chief executives, a single slate under one arm, screen dark. Mid-to-late forties, pale from a life spent mostly under building light instead of daylight, dark hair graying at the edges like he'd arranged it, though nothing else about him suggested he arranged anything. Analysts didn't look up as he passed. Not fear, Malcolm thought, just people used to him being there.

AFTER (revised per feedback — kept the hair detail, merged with the tie observation instead of cutting it):
> Adrian Vale crossed the floor without the cluster of aides Malcolm associated with chief executives, a single slate under one arm, screen dark. His tie was still knotted tight enough to mean something at an hour when everyone else's had loosened, and his hair, graying at the edges, looked arranged the same way, though nothing else about him suggested he ever needed to try. Analysts didn't look up as he passed. Not fear, Malcolm thought, just people used to him being there.

**Heritage choice:** dropped the age/pale checklist items, but restored the hair detail per feedback — merged it with the tie observation into one image (his controlled, arranged exterior) instead of two separate checklist facts. Caucasian-American heritage left unstated here, same reasoning as Beck. **Resolved:** no deferred beat added elsewhere; this version stands as the full fix.

## 3. Daniel Cho (Ch23)

BEFORE:
> The room had no windows and no clock, which Daniel understood immediately as a decision rather than an oversight. Fifty-one, Korean-American, running soft through the middle the way men do when a desk replaces the fieldwork that used to keep them lean. Twenty years of compliance work had left him with good posture and nothing else athletic about him.

AFTER:
> The room had no windows and no clock, which Daniel understood immediately as a decision rather than an oversight. Twenty years of compliance work had left him with good posture and nothing else athletic about him. Whatever had kept him lean in the field had gone soft behind a desk long before this room found a use for him.

**Heritage choice:** this was the worst offender (age + ethnicity stated as a bare label in one clause) so I cut both here. **Resolved per feedback:** left contextual/unstated, no deferred beat — same treatment Markou already gets in this book (name + context only). No further changes needed.

BEFORE:
> Naomi Kincaid said it from the doorway, and everyone in the metrics meeting had time to look guilty before she reached the table. Thirty-two, brown skin, dark hair already losing the fight it always lost by afternoon.

AFTER:
> Naomi Kincaid said it from the doorway, and everyone in the metrics meeting had time to look guilty before she reached the table. Brown skin, dark hair already losing the fight it always lost by afternoon.

**Heritage choice:** kept skin tone (unlike Beck/Adrian, dropping it here would leave her with zero physical/heritage marker anywhere in the book — checked, and the project reference material's plan to carry her Trinidadian heritage through a vocal tell under stress was never actually written into the manuscript, so skin tone is currently the only place it lives on the page). Only cut is "Thirty-two" — the bare age number, lowest-value part of the sentence, cut for rhythm more than formula-breaking.

## 6. Elif Karaca (Ch13)

BEFORE:
> Elif Karaca sat at the end of a crowded table with three tenants, two children, and a municipal inspection report. Early forties, olive-skinned, one streak of premature gray at her temple, dressed in a suit chosen for how many hours it could survive rather than how it photographed.

AFTER:
> Elif Karaca sat at the end of a crowded table with three tenants, two children, and a municipal inspection report, one streak of gray at her temple she'd earned faster than she expected to and never bothered coloring. She wore the same kind of suit every day — chosen for how many hours it could survive, not how it photographed.

**Heritage choice:** dropped "Early forties, olive-skinned" as bare labels. Unlike Beck/Adrian, this isn't really a "defer or drop" call — Elif's Turkish identity is already carried extremely strongly by context the moment she appears (Istanbul setting, Turkish name, addressed in Turkish by others, parliamentary role), stronger contextual grounding than any other character in the book gets. Folded the gray-streak detail into the action clause instead of a separate inventory sentence.
