# Pilot — Cold Read Follow-up: Framework/Istanbul agreement naming inconsistency

**Source:** original cold-read report §2 — "Eastern Mediterranean Framework" / "Istanbul agreement" / "the agreement" used near-interchangeably across Ch13-27, "a small re-orientation cost each time it recurred." Flagged again in the follow-up read as still present, never promoted to the ranked top-10.

## Survey

Five distinct textual forms found for the same treaty across the book:
1. **"the Istanbul agreement"** — Elif/Naomi's usage, first appears Ch13, used 3x.
2. **"the Eastern Mediterranean agreement"** — first appears Ch16, used 3x (Ch16, Ch18's clarifying sentence, Ch28/29 manifesto).
3. **"the Eastern Mediterranean infrastructure agreement"** — one-off, Ch18.
4. **bare "the Framework"** — Tunalı's shorthand, Ch19 and Ch27.
5. **"EASTERN MEDITERRANEAN FRAMEWORK [RATIFICATION/CONFERENCE]"** — official document-header form, Ch19/20 and Ch29.

**Real problem found, not just "loose naming":** there's already a deliberate clarifying sentence in Ch18 ("the Eastern Mediterranean agreement, the same cross-border pact Elif called the Istanbul agreement") explicitly acknowledging two names for one treaty — a legitimate, realistic touch (different political contexts genuinely do name the same treaty differently) worth keeping, not eliminating. But it uses variant #2, not the official "Framework" form, and Tunalı's bare "the Framework" (variant #4, Ch19) is used *before* the full "Eastern Mediterranean Framework" name is ever established in running prose (that doesn't happen until later in the same chapter, variant #5). A reader hits the shorthand before the thing it's short for.

## Approach

Keep exactly two names, both already established and doing real work: **"the Istanbul agreement"** (political/colloquial, Elif and Naomi's usage) and **"the Eastern Mediterranean Framework"** (the official name, which Tunalı's "the Framework" naturally shortens once established). Fold variants #2 and #3 into #5, which also fixes the ordering problem — the full name now appears in Ch16, well before Ch19's shorthand.

## Changes (4 edits, all "agreement" → "Framework")

### 1. Ch16 (line ~4139)
BEFORE: "The contract would become part of the Eastern Mediterranean agreement's shared incident-response system after the Istanbul conference."
AFTER: "The contract would become part of the Eastern Mediterranean Framework's shared incident-response system after the Istanbul conference."

### 2. Ch18, the clarifying sentence (line ~4479)
BEFORE: "The search also kept returning the Eastern Mediterranean agreement, the same cross-border pact Elif called the Istanbul agreement."
AFTER: "The search also kept returning the Eastern Mediterranean Framework, the same cross-border pact Elif called the Istanbul agreement."

### 3. Ch18 (line ~4681)
BEFORE: "Elif added the contractor to her review of the Eastern Mediterranean infrastructure agreement."
AFTER: "Elif added the contractor to her review of the Eastern Mediterranean Framework."

### 4. Ch28/29, manifesto text (line ~8276)
BEFORE: "The manifesto condemned the Eastern Mediterranean agreement, foreign control of maritime security, energy corridors, and the surrender of Turkish infrastructure to hostile states."
AFTER: "The manifesto condemned the Eastern Mediterranean Framework, foreign control of maritime security, energy corridors, and the surrender of Turkish infrastructure to hostile states."

## Not touched

- **"the Istanbul agreement"** (3x) — kept as-is, Elif's deliberate colloquial usage, explicitly contrasted with the official name in the Ch18 clarifying sentence.
- **Bare "the Framework"** (Tunalı, Ch19/Ch27) — kept as-is; now correctly follows the full name's first appearance instead of preceding it.
- **"EASTERN MEDITERRANEAN FRAMEWORK [RATIFICATION/CONFERENCE]"** document headers — already consistent, not touched.
- **"The Eastern Mediterranean meeting in Istanbul"** (Ch27) — a descriptive phrase, not a proper-noun variant, no change needed.

## Self-audit

- All four edits are single-word swaps ("agreement"/"infrastructure agreement" → "Framework"), no sentence restructuring, no new information.
- Rechecked chronological order after the edits: "Eastern Mediterranean Framework" now first appears Ch16 (line 4139), well before Tunalı's first bare "the Framework" in Ch19 — ordering problem resolved.
- The realistic two-name texture (official name vs. political colloquialism) is preserved, not flattened to one name.
