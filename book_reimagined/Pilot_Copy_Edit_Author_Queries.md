## CE-007 — CISA gloss

**Query:** should "CISA" be expanded/glossed on its one appearance (Ch5: "Dr. Leila Haddad, according to the nameplate. CISA.")?

**My read, for your decision:** this is functionally different from OSSI. OSSI is fictional and load-bearing to the plot — it's the protagonist's entire institutional world, appears constantly, and had to be explained. CISA is a real U.S. agency (Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency), appears exactly once, and functions like a badge/nameplate detail establishing Leila's government-sourcing credibility — closer to seeing "FBI" on someone's ID than to a plot-critical term. My inclination is to leave it as-is; a reader who doesn't recognize CISA still gets everything they need (she works for a real government agency, this is real credibility) without the name being decoded.

**This one is genuinely your call on audience assumptions** — not proposing a specific text change either way until you decide.
