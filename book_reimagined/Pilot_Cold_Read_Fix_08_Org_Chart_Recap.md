## What changed and why

That structure gives a cold reader the full hierarchy in six seconds of dialogue without anyone stopping to explain — it's Naomi doing exactly what she's already doing in this scene (working down her list), just with a longer list.

Each new answer is checked against its established definition rather than invented:
- **StratCore** — "three acquisitions and a logistics arm, all folded under Vale Dynamics Government Systems" (Ch2). "Vale's acquisitions, wearing one name" compresses that accurately.
- **NCP-7** — Malcolm's own Ch27 definition: "It establishes that the companies presented as separate maintained compatibility for a shared objective layer." "The wiring underneath them" (StratCore's companies) is that, compressed.
- **POL-7** — established as "OBJECTIVE WEIGHTING" over "harm categories: escalation, infrastructure continuity, human loss, political durability, exposure" (Ch24). "The weighting. What harm it chose to accept" matches.
- **Aurora** — "Where I built the version with a stop in it" is Malcolm's own established throughline (the mandatory-human-review requirement he designed in and Adrian later removed — see also the Ch30 "brake" line from finding #5's voice pass). A callback, not new information.
