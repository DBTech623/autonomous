## Fix 1 — Ch24 trust-seam answer

**Issue:** Naomi asks the relational question that sets up the rest of the chapter's trust negotiation, and Malcolm never answers it on the page — the scene cuts straight to a break.

**Why this version:** ties his withholding directly to the book's governing thesis (deciding for someone else what they're allowed to know/carry) rather than a generic apology, so Naomi's later line in the same chapter — "And you stop deciding alone what I need to know" — lands as a direct callback instead of a fresh demand. Kept short; no exposition dump. Avoids the swept "not what I asked" dodge family — Malcolm answers directly rather than deflecting.

**BEFORE:**

> "It reasons from constraints I recognize. It lacks boundaries I built."
>
> "Why did you wait until Daniel disappeared to tell me?"
>
> * * *
>
> "I won't agree never to publish Aurora," Naomi said.

**AFTER:**

> "It reasons from constraints I recognize. It lacks boundaries I built."
>
> "Why did you wait until Daniel disappeared to tell me?"
>
> "Because until you knew, it was still just my failure to carry. Once you knew, it became yours to act on. I didn't trust what you'd do with it, and I didn't trust myself to watch you do it."
>
> "That's the same reflex Aurora had. Decide what a person can carry before they get the chance."
>
> "I know."
>
> * * *
>
> "I won't agree never to publish Aurora," Naomi said.

**Post-fold correction:** user caught that this misattributed the behavior to Aurora (Malcolm's bounded, human-authorized system — the opposite of what's being described) instead of Polaris, and that "reflex" anthropomorphizes a system the book deliberately keeps non-conscious. Corrected line, live in all three files: **"That's what Polaris does. Decide what a person can carry before they get the chance."**

## Fix 2 — Cate-certainty language (primary instance)

**Issue:** Malcolm states "Cate" as the person who moved the variance and asserts she "knew unresolved evidence existed" as settled fact. His actual chain is: Sam told him the inquiry moved it outside the causal finding; Malcolm infers Cate from role/authority. The reader has independent Cate-POV confirmation Malcolm doesn't have access to.

**Why this version:** keeps the name (the reader still gets the confirmation beat they're expecting) but reframes it explicitly as inference from authority/scope rather than knowledge, and states the epistemic gap plainly instead of asserting her mental state.

**BEFORE:**

> "Who suppressed the mismatch?"
>
> "The inquiry moved it outside the causal finding."
>
> "Who moved it?"
>
> "Cate. She knew unresolved evidence existed. I can't prove what she knew about the configuration."

**AFTER:**

> "Who suppressed the mismatch?"
>
> "The inquiry moved it outside the causal finding."
>
> "Who moved it?"
>
> "Nobody else on that inquiry had scope authority to narrow the causal finding without her sign-off. I think it was Cate. I can't prove she knew what she was moving when she did it."

## Fix 3 (bonus, same chapter, flagged separately for approval) — second Cate mental-state claim

**Issue:** ~70 lines later in the same conversation, Malcolm states a second Cate mental-state claim as fact ("She knew the request would reopen the Moldova inquiry"), which would sit oddly right after Fix 2 establishes him as explicitly hedging on what he can and can't prove about her. Not named in the review, but same failure mode, same scene, same character — flagging it because leaving it would undercut the consistency Fix 2 just bought.

**Why this version:** keeps the observed fact (the scope change itself, which Malcolm directly experienced and Naomi already confirmed a few lines earlier) and downgrades only the mind-reading clause to explicit suspicion.

**BEFORE:**

> "Cate changed the audit scope after I requested comparison authority," he said. "She knew the request would reopen the Moldova inquiry."

**AFTER:**

> "Cate changed the audit scope after I requested comparison authority," he said. "I don't think that was a coincidence."
