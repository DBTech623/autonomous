## 1. Ch11 — the isolated diagnostic environment (LE-065)

BEFORE: `The isolated diagnostic environment had no connection to client systems — no network path in or out, air-gapped by design so nothing inside it could leave at all. Its walls were bare, its console old, and its cooling fans loud enough to make conversation unpleasant. He had chosen it years ago because no executive enjoyed visiting, and because a physical gap left nothing to misconfigure — no firewall rule to except, no permission to grant by mistake.`

AFTER: `The isolated diagnostic environment had no connection to client systems — no network path in or out, air-gapped by design so nothing inside it could leave at all.

Its walls were bare, its console old, and its cooling fans loud enough to make conversation unpleasant. He had chosen it years ago because no executive enjoyed visiting, and because a physical gap left nothing to misconfigure — no firewall rule to except, no permission to grant by mistake.`

**What changed:** split network topology from room description + Adrian's rationale. Kept both em-dashes — each is doing real appositive work (elaborating "no connection" and "nothing to misconfigure"), not decorative.

## 2. Ch11 — "the identity" (LE-066)

BEFORE: `The identity had entered the isolated environment during the inherited architecture validation. Security policy recommended deletion. Adrian opened the credential history.`

AFTER: `Malcolm's legacy credential had entered the isolated environment during the inherited architecture validation. Security policy recommended deletion. Adrian opened the credential history.`

**What changed:** named the credential concretely instead of the abstract "the identity."

## 3. Ch11 — "already reached only" (LE-067)

BEFORE: `Adrian deferred the deletion recommendation for thirty days under legacy validation review. The identity already reached only the isolated environment, so he left its boundary unchanged.`

AFTER: `Adrian deferred the deletion recommendation for thirty days under legacy validation review. The identity was already scoped to the isolated environment, so he left its boundary unchanged.`

**What changed:** "reached only" isn't idiomatic for credential scope; "scoped to" is the correct technical term.

## 4. Ch11 — Reddick's call (LE-069)

He'd spent eleven years at the Defense Intelligence Agency learning how a sentence survived a congressional hearing, and the nine since learning that Vale rarely convened one.`

AFTER: `Reddick called at the hour he always called, the one that let him say he'd tried Adrian before end of day without risking an actual conversation with an assistant.

He'd spent eleven years at the Defense Intelligence Agency learning how a sentence survived a congressional hearing, and the nine since learning that Vale rarely convened one.`

**What changed:** split character business (why he calls at this hour) from backstory (his DIA history) into separate paragraphs.

## 5. Ch12 — the audit-room opening (LE-072)

BEFORE: `Malcolm watched Cate read it from the far end of the audit table, where she'd asked to sit in once Torres flagged the draft's word choice to her the night before. Torres turned a paper label between his fingers. Leila looked at her normalized timeline instead of the sentence.`

AFTER: `Malcolm watched Cate read it from the far end of the audit table, where she'd asked to sit in once Torres flagged the draft's word choice to her the night before.

Torres turned a paper label between his fingers. Leila looked at her normalized timeline instead of the sentence.`

**What changed:** split so the reader lands on Cate's presence first, then Torres/Leila, instead of all three landing in one breath.

## 6. Ch12 — the verification-sheet sentence (LE-073)

BEFORE: `Malcolm read the verification sheet first, then checked the time-zone declaration — UTC — and the signing chain: a Vardonian infrastructure liaison had transmitted the order to the parliamentary inquiry under a bilateral oversight request tied to the regional carrier consortium both countries shared. Turkish parliamentary counsel had verified the signature against the public key named in the liaison agreement.`

AFTER: `Malcolm read the verification sheet first, then checked the time-zone declaration, UTC, and the signing chain. A Vardonian infrastructure liaison had transmitted the order to the parliamentary inquiry under a bilateral oversight request tied to the regional carrier consortium both countries shared.

Turkish parliamentary counsel had verified the signature against the public key named in the liaison agreement.`

**What changed:** split the evidentiary chain into two units (what Malcolm checked, then what the record shows) and removed both em-dashes around "UTC" — a three-letter value doesn't need dash emphasis, commas carry it fine.

## 7. Ch13 — leadership convenes (LE-075)

BEFORE: `Leadership had convened on four hours' notice to review the audit's language, in a conference room that required two badges at the door and screens sunk into the table, already lit at every seat when Malcolm walked in.`

AFTER: `Leadership had convened on four hours' notice to review the audit's language. The conference room required two badges at the door. Screens were sunk into the table, already lit at every seat when Malcolm walked in.`

**What changed:** split the meeting's purpose from the room description, so the institutional pressure of the chapter opening doesn't get delayed by a nested location clause. Also fixed a zeugma the user caught — "required" naturally governs "two badges" (an access rule) but not "screens sunk into the table" (a physical feature, not a requirement); that was already latent in the original phrasing, my first split just isolated and exposed it. Split "required" off from the screens description entirely.

## 8. Ch13 — Cate's warning (LE-076)

BEFORE: `"Mine," she said. "A finding attributed to an analyst on a temporary, purpose-bound assignment, one carrying the kind of history yours does, invites a different kind of scrutiny than one attributed to the audit team as a whole. That scrutiny lands on you first. It does not stay there."`

AFTER: `"Mine," she said. "A finding attributed to an analyst on a temporary, purpose-bound assignment, one carrying the kind of history yours does, invites a different kind of scrutiny than one attributed to the audit team as a whole."

She let the sentence settle. "That scrutiny lands on you first. It does not stay there."`

**What changed:** added a short beat between the two halves of Cate's speech so "It does not stay there" lands with more force. Checked first whether the manuscript uses continuous multi-paragraph quotes from one speaker (no closing/reopening quote mark) anywhere — it doesn't, so I used a real action beat instead of introducing an unprecedented dialogue-formatting convention for one line.

Naomi caught the next flight and went straight from the airport to Esenyurt, a crowded working-class district on Istanbul's western edge, running on airport coffee and no sleep.`

AFTER: `Naomi had asked Elif's office for an interview every week since Zeynep's first message about the Vardonian order. This time, Elif said yes. The story had found an audience in Turkey nobody at the paper had planned for, and she wanted the interview done in person, not over a connection either government could interrupt.

Naomi caught the next flight and went straight from the airport to Esenyurt, a crowded working-class district on Istanbul's western edge, running on airport coffee and no sleep.`

**What changed:** split the request history/rationale from the actual travel and arrival.

## 10. Ch14 — Elif's two aphorisms (LE-081)

BEFORE: `"I could have, but when my schedule allows I like to see to these things myself. People take some comfort in watching someone from their government show up in person. So do I." She shifted the medicine bag to her other arm. "Government is supposed to serve people. Not the reverse."`

AFTER: `"I could have, but when my schedule allows I like to see to these things myself. People take some comfort in watching someone from their government show up in person. So do I." She shifted the medicine bag to her other arm.`

**What changed:** cut the trailing "Government is supposed to serve people. Not the reverse." — both statements were making the same point about her governing philosophy, and this one read as more of a summarized thesis. Kept the first (with "So do I," which needs its antecedent) so the beat ends on the more personal, scene-grounded note rather than the abstract maxim.

## 11. Ch14 — "you learn to notice" (LE-083)

BEFORE: `"I grew up near Anamur with six siblings, in a house with two bedrooms. You learn to notice who's hungry before they say anything."`

AFTER: `"I grew up near Anamur with six siblings, in a house with two bedrooms. You learn fast who hasn't eaten."`

**What changed:** shortened the second sentence so it reads as something said in conversation rather than a polished maxim — several of Elif's lines already land as composed aphorisms, so this one gets a plainer, more clipped shape.
