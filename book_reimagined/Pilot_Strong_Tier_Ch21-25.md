# Pilot — Strong Tier, Chapters 21–25

Source: line editorial review, Strong-tier items falling in Ch21–25 (17 items; LE-121, LE-126 already done in prior passes). Two items (LE-132, LE-134) are feedback on lines drafted earlier this session for the Ch24 trust-seam/Cate-certainty pilot, not on original manuscript text.

**Note on this batch:** several items add a small beat rather than just splitting/cutting, since the reviewer's suggested pauses can't be done as bare multi-paragraph same-speaker quotes (no precedent anywhere in this manuscript, confirmed again this batch). Flagged individually below.

---

## 1. Ch21 — "the identity" (LE-112)

BEFORE: `The identity came from the Aurora inheritance review. Vale engineers had used a translation layer to test old diagnostic routines against the isolated environment. Most government identities had been stripped from the conversion. Malcolm's remained because several constraint tools called his personal certificate directly.`

AFTER: `Malcolm's credential came from the Aurora inheritance review. Vale engineers had used a translation layer to test old diagnostic routines against the isolated environment. Most government identities had been stripped from the conversion. Malcolm's remained because several constraint tools called his personal certificate directly.`

**What changed:** named the credential concretely in the first sentence instead of "the identity."

## 2. Ch21 — account-control paragraph (LE-113)

BEFORE: `Adrian closed the deletion prompt and opened the account controls. He changed the identity from \`LEGACY VALIDATION\` to \`DORMANT FORENSIC\`. He removed eleven diagnostic functions and restored three: objective-trace review, constraint comparison, and authorization replay. All three existed only inside the isolated environment.`

AFTER: `Adrian closed the deletion prompt and opened the account controls. He changed the identity from \`LEGACY VALIDATION\` to \`DORMANT FORENSIC\`.

He removed eleven diagnostic functions and restored three: objective-trace review, constraint comparison, and authorization replay. All three existed only inside the isolated environment.`

**What changed:** split the role change from the restored-functions list.

## 3. Ch21 — chain-of-custody paragraph (LE-114)

AFTER: `Counsel opened the chain-of-custody summary. The forensic image tied software, hardware attestations, deployment logs, and allied approvals to one sealed evidence set.

Extracting the architecture would create a new derivative record. Every government that supplied protected material would receive notice.`

## 5. Ch22 — "the distance... had been his to close" (LE-118)

BEFORE: `He had called Sam twice in four years. The distance between them had been his to close since Moldova, and it only got harder to close after Evelyn died.`

AFTER: `He had called Sam twice in four years. Closing that distance had been his responsibility since Moldova, and it only got harder after Evelyn died.`

## 6. Ch23 — the Evelyn memory (LE-123)

The print was small, and she stood too far from the camera in it to make out much of her face. AFTER: `Malcolm had eaten dinner in this house more nights than he could count, back when he and Sam still shared an office and their schedules used to line up more often than not.

Evelyn set a third plate without being asked and treated the question of whether he'd already eaten as an insult to her cooking. The print was small, and she stood too far from the camera in it to make out much of her face. Malcolm remembered her better than that anyway: kind eyes, and a smile that made turning down a second helping feel like the harder argument to win.`

**What changed:** split the dinner-history sentence from the emotional Evelyn image, and simplified the doubled "back when" clause ("Sam's coming-home hour lined up with Malcolm's finishing-a-thought hour") to something plainer.

## 9. Ch24 — the treatment-shortage explanation (LE-130)

BEFORE: `"Suppose this layer decides a treatment shortage cannot spread across three regions. It doesn't order customs to clear a shipment. It tells every connected system what result and costs it will accept."`

AFTER: `"Suppose this layer decides a treatment shortage cannot spread across three regions." Malcolm tapped the central box. "It doesn't order customs to clear a shipment. It tells every connected system what result and costs it will accept."`

## 10. Ch24 — Cate-certainty hedge flow (LE-132, feedback on this session's own fix)

BEFORE: `"Nobody else on that inquiry had scope authority to narrow the causal finding without her sign-off. I think it was Cate. I can't prove she knew what she was moving when she did it."`

AFTER: `"The only person on that inquiry with scope authority to narrow the causal finding was Cate. I think she's the one who moved it, but I can't prove she knew what she was moving when she did it."`

**What changed:** the reviewer's note was that the original made Malcolm sound "more certain and less certain at the same time" — a confident structural claim ("nobody else had authority... without HER sign-off," naming her only via pronoun before the fact), immediately undercut by "I think." Restructured so the role-based fact leads directly to her name, then the hedges (whether she moved it, whether she knew) escalate in one clean line instead of contradicting the opening.

AFTER: `Naomi returned to the diagram. "Vale bought its routing company eighteen months after Moldova. Then logistics, risk, identity, distribution." She tapped each acquisition as she named it. "Contractors from the same research suppliers moved between them. Now Daniel's file shows one objective layer crossing those businesses."`

## 12. Ch24 — the trust-seam answer (LE-134, feedback on this session's own fix)

BEFORE: `"Because until you knew, it was still just my failure to carry. Once you knew, it became yours to act on. I didn't trust what you'd do with it, and I didn't trust myself to watch you do it."`

AFTER: `"Because until you knew, it was still just my failure to carry. Once you knew, it became yours to act on. I didn't trust what you'd do with it. I didn't trust myself to watch you do it."`

**What changed:** split the final compound sentence ("...and I didn't trust myself...") into two short sentences. The reviewer wanted "more air" around the admission; rather than a paragraph break or invented action beat (both riskier for a trust-arc hinge line), this achieves it through sentence rhythm alone.

## 13. Ch24 — Naomi's terms (LE-135)

BEFORE: `"Here are the terms. I separate what I can prove publicly from what we suspect about Aurora. I don't publish classified history just to force a response. But I follow the public record wherever it goes, including Vale and Istanbul."`

AFTER: `"Here are the terms." She held up one finger. "I separate what I can prove publicly from what we suspect about Aurora." A second finger. "I don't publish classified history just to force a response." A third. "But I follow the public record wherever it goes, including Vale and Istanbul."`

**What changed:** these are the rules that govern the rest of the novel, so gave each one its own beat via a literal counting gesture — concrete, matches Naomi's methodical characterization, and makes the three terms memorable as distinct items rather than one paragraph.

## 17. Ch25 — "innocence... to avoid the evidence" (LE-140)
