## Not applied

**LE-039 (Ch6) — reviewer's suggested fix is itself the grammar error.** Original: `Malcolm watched Leila stop looking for evasion and start looking for error, and not finding either.` The reviewer suggests changing "either" to "neither," but "not finding either" is standard, correct English (equivalent in meaning to "finding neither" — compare "she didn't find either answer convincing"). Changing it to "not finding neither" would introduce a double negative. Recommend leaving this line exactly as-is.
