## Draft Two Timeline and Continuity Pass (Revision Plan Pass 6)

**Status:** Complete
**Scope:** All 32 finalized v3 chapter files

This pass builds the working calendar the revision plan calls for and checks it against the seven specific continuity risks the plan names. It supersedes nothing in the earlier Draft Two Continuity Audit — it extends that audit's timeline-anchors table into a full relative calendar and re-verifies it against the v3 text now that Passes 1–5 and 8 (dialogue-tic cleanup) have touched nearly every chapter.

The manuscript deliberately uses relative intervals ("forty-seven minutes," "thirty-two hours," "four days") rather than calendar dates. This calendar preserves that choice — it anchors everything to Day 0 (the Baltic correction) rather than inventing dates the prose doesn't use.

## Check-list verification (Pass 6's seven items)

**Travel time** — Naomi departs "tomorrow evening" (Ch. 24) and is credentialed and on-site by Ch. 25/26. An overnight transatlantic flight fits the gap cleanly. No fix needed.

**Source contact intervals** — Daniel's calls (Ch. 15 first contact, Ch. 16 second call and door intrusion, Ch. 21 missed meeting) carry their own internal clock (a scheduled six-hour meeting window, a twenty-seven-minute-late check-in, explicit 6:32/7:05/7:10/7:11 timestamps at the missed meeting). All consistent. No fix needed.

**Audit approval and appeal time** — Ch. 17 ends with Torres agreeing to schedule a closed leadership review; Ch. 19 is that review. Ch. 23's suspension follows Malcolm's unauthorized retrieval attempt directly, same day. No contradiction found.

**Conference credential timing** — Elif tells Naomi in Ch. 21 that her office "accredited you as a policy journalist three days ago," meaning Elif began that paperwork back around the Ch. 15–16 window — consistent with her own line in Ch. 21 ("You said maybe. In politics that means begin the paperwork.").

**Daniel's missed check-ins** — Explicit and internally consistent (6:32 first check-in, 7:05/7:10 deadline, 7:11 package release) in Ch. 21.

**Four-hour Istanbul response** — The "four hours" figure in the earlier Continuity Audit is a summary estimate, not a number stated in-text. The actual textual anchors are the ~90-minute pre-departure contamination window (Ch. 26) and the embargo/countdown beats in Ch. 27–31 (six-minute embargo timer, forty-two-second/six-minute containment clocks in Ch. 28, twelve-second gap between the Polaris name going live and Naomi's article publishing in Ch. 31). These are all internally consistent with each other; nothing in the prose actually claims a single four-hour span, so there's no contradiction to fix — just a loose paraphrase in the older audit document.

**Public inquiry timing** — Cate's `LIMITED INQUIRY` order (Ch. 31) and its visible effects (Turkish inquiry dropping the Elif-as-target framing, Vale losing/reviewing contracts) in Ch. 32 progress in the right order with no timing collision.

## Fix applied

Chapter 32 lacked the single elapsed-time statement the revision plan calls for ("State the elapsed time since publication once. Do not add a broad epilogue" — Pass 5, Ch. 32). The chapter's middle section implied *some* passage of time (four partner investigations, a shifted official narrative, a memorial committee already being organized) without ever pinning down how much. Added one clause at the top of that section:

> "Eleven days after publication, Naomi called while Malcolm was drawing a box above Vale."

Eleven days is long enough to justify the institutional movement already described (contract losses, a changed official position, a memorial committee) and short enough that Zeynep's live worry — "The public attention will move on" — still reads as a real risk rather than something already settled. This is the only elapsed-time claim in the chapter; the final newsroom scene deliberately keeps its own timing loose ("before dinner" reads as *that day*, not publication day) rather than adding a second, competing marker.

## Outcome

No plot-timing contradictions were found across the v3 files. The one gap (Chapter 32's unstated elapsed time) is now fixed. The manuscript's relative-interval style is preserved throughout — this pass adds one anchor, not a calendar overlay.
