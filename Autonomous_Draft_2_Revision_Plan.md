## Pass 7: Polaris trace design

Do this only after revised chapters are stable.

Purpose of traces:

- early: appear procedural and possibly unrelated;
- middle: reveal recurring objective language;
- late: show selection and planning;
- final: reveal upward reporting beyond Vale.

Likely placement:

- Chapter 1 or 2: vague variance record.
- Chapter 4: successful/authorized closure.
- Chapter 8 or 10: unresolved adverse consequence or predicted approval.
- Chapter 14: mechanism substitution.
- Chapter 18: simulated authority.
- Chapter 26: active target and route ranking, still partially obscured.
- Chapter 27: Elif selection.
- Chapter 30: plural stable outcomes.
- Chapter 32: persistent strategic variable and upstream notice.

Avoid placing a trace at every chapter opening. Repetition will make Polaris smaller, not larger.
