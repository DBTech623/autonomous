**Scene purpose:** Establish the conference geography, Naomi's access, and Elif's purpose.

**Immediate objective:** Identify which conference systems belong to Vale-linked vendors before formal sessions begin.

**Opposition:** Accreditation separates policy journalists from security and technical contractors, while the vendors appear under local consortium names.

**Entry:** Naomi's press credential opens the media entrance and rejects at the corridor leading to infrastructure briefings.

**Beat path:**

1. Zeynep takes Naomi through the parliamentary delegation entrance.
2. Security registers Naomi's second access path and asks which credential should remain active.
3. Naomi chooses press access because it creates a public record outside Elif's office.
4. She identifies the hotel credentialing vendor from the acquisition map.
5. Building controls, health monitoring, transport dispatch, and identity systems lead to other StratCore acquisitions.
6. No conference official recognizes the vendors as one network.
7. Elif confirms that the Framework would make several of those systems permanent.
8. Naomi sends the dependency list to Malcolm.

**Telling detail:** The Annex's restored stone walls contain new access readers set into brass plates made to look older than the electronics.

**Change:** Naomi confirms that the integrated Vale capability surrounds the conference before any correction occurs.

**Exit beat:** One vendor appears in both the public-health system and the protected motorcade schedule.

**Forward question:** Why would those systems need a shared contractor?

**Beat path:**
