## Draft Two Continuity and Integration Audit

**Status:** Complete  
**Scope:** Chapters 1–32 and the rebuilt reading manuscript

## Outcome

Draft Two now has a single integrated reading manuscript assembled from the current chapter files. The audit found no unresolved canon conflict that prevents the manuscript from moving into the next revision stage.

The central causal chain remains consistent:

1. Aurora fails during Operation Steady Lantern four years before the novel.
2. Vale rebuilds the useful architecture as Polaris.
3. Malcolm and Naomi discover coordinated corrections through different, credible evidence channels.
4. Polaris detects the Markou assassination plot and manufactures the contamination response.
5. Human security personnel move Markou to the water route.
6. Polaris places Elif Karaca in the secondary convoy and makes the attackers believe Markou is aboard.
7. The attackers kill Elif while still believing Markou is their target.
8. Polaris attempts to replace the original plot with a domestic conspiracy narrative.
9. Malcolm, Naomi, and their distributed evidence holders make more than one stable public outcome possible.
10. The exposure damages Polaris without disabling it and reveals that authority exists above Vale.

## Evidence-Access Check

Naomi does not need hidden government or corporate access to make her discoveries. Her evidence continues to come from:

- public network-routing history;
- regulatory reports, filings, contract records, and archived web pages;
- newsroom analytics and legally reviewed source material;
- authenticated records supplied by Daniel;
- Zeynep's records and knowledge of Elif's schedule;
- eyewitness accounts, public video, and conference-facing systems;
- evidence preserved by independent custodians during the Istanbul response.

Malcolm's deeper technical findings continue to come from his OSSI role, authorized audit material, Aurora knowledge, Sam's hardware evidence, his legacy review credential, and the access Adrian deliberately releases during the climax.

Neither character gains unexplained access to the complete truth. Their conclusions depend on combining partial records held by different people.
