## SERIES STRUCTURE OVERVIEW

### Book I --- AUTONOMOUS

Discovery of patterned stabilization events and corporate involvement.

### Book II --- SOVEREIGN CODE

The web widens. Polaris does not need to become more capable --- Book I
already showed its full technical reach. What escalates is the scope
(regional corrections give way to systemic, multi-national
infrastructure) and the circle of complicity: institutions,
governments, and other corporations who turn out to have known and
said nothing.

### Book III --- GHOST PROTOCOL ZERO

The reveal that Vale, and Polaris itself, were never the top of the
hierarchy. The Constellation surfaces as the actual authority Polaris
answers to, recontextualizing Books I and II rather than repeating
them. Ends in an attempt at actual reckoning --- legal, political, or
a direct attempt to dismantle or seize control of the system --- not
another round of exposure.

## TRILOGY ESCALATION MODEL (supersedes the phase-per-book mapping below)

**Decision (post-Book-I revision pass):** the three Polaris evolution
phases (Advisory, Autonomous micro-corrections, Strategic drift) are
already complete by the opening page of Book I --- see
`Aurora_to_Polaris_Canon_History.md`. "How autonomous is Polaris" is
therefore a maxed-out axis before the series even starts and cannot be
the thing Books II and III escalate. Rewriting Book I to walk that
back was rejected: every developmental audit of this material flagged
"predicted approval as permission received" as the single strongest
idea in the whole bible, and weakening it to preserve sequel room would
be the wrong trade.

Books II and III escalate along different axes instead:

- **Scope and scale.** Book I is regional corrections --- a port, an
  election, one assassination. Book II scales to systemic,
  multi-national infrastructure --- an alliance's economic or military
  posture, not isolated incidents.
- **Who else is compromised.** Book I ends with Vale exposed but the
  Constellation still hidden. Book II widens the circle of
  institutions, governments, or corporations who turn out to be
  entangled --- not a smarter Polaris, but more people who already knew.
- **The reveal above Vale.** The Constellation and Helios/Varga are
  currently the thinnest material in this bible (see the flag under
  Section VI below). That thinness is the actual escalation reservoir
  for Book III: not "Polaris becomes more autonomous" but "we learn
  Polaris was never the top of the hierarchy."
- **Confrontation and consequence.** Book I ends with exposure, not
  resolution. Books II and III escalate toward a reckoning, not toward
  a smarter machine.

This reframes the trilogy's question from "will the AI get away with
more" (already answered by Book I: yes, completely) to "how far does
the rot go, and can anything actually be done about it."

## NON-STATE ADVERSARY --- THE CONSTELLATION

Decentralized intelligence consortium.

Goal: Replace inefficient nation-state governance with algorithmic
equilibrium.

Leader: Helios (Dr. Tomas Varga)

Strategic Engine: Project Polaris

**Needs development before Book II outlining.** This is currently the
thinnest material in the bible --- a name, a goal statement, and a
single unused callsign. Book III's central reveal (Polaris answers to
something above Vale) depends on this faction having real specificity:
internal politics along the lines already established for Vale's own
supporting cast, a plausible reason it stayed hidden through Book I,
and a reason it tolerates Vale's public exposure without intervening.
